The Vicsek model: order from noise and alignment

The physicist's minimal flock (Vicsek et al. 1995). Every particle moves at the same speed and, each step, takes the average heading of the particles within one unit of it (itself included), then turns by a random angle up to η/2 either way. Nothing else: no separation, no cohesion. At low noise they move together; at high noise they wander. Colour is heading. The box wraps. docs/15-swarm.md, S2.

Order

va = |Σ vi| / (N v): 1 when every particle heads the same way; for random headings √π/(2√N), not 1/√N.

The transition

The paper's Fig. 2a, measured here: for each η, fresh systems of N = 40, 100, 400 and 4000 at the paper's density (ρ ≈ 4), 1,000 steps discarded, then va averaged over 1,000 more. As N grows the drop sharpens and moves toward the paper's ηc ≈ 2.9. Later, larger simulations (Grégoire & Chaté 2004; Chaté et al. 2008) found the transition is in fact discontinuous, which these sizes can't show. N = 4000 takes most of the ~45 s.

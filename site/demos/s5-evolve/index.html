Evolving the rules: hand-tuned ants against evolved ones

S3's ants run on seven constants (how far ahead and how wide they sense, how hard they turn, how much they wander, how their trails fade and spread). Their defaults came from a hand scan. Here an evolutionary algorithm searched all seven, scoring each candidate by the food a colony brings home in 3,000 steps, natively and in parallel. Left: the hand-tuned defaults. Right: the evolved setting. Same scene, same seed, same 500 ants. docs/15-swarm.md, S5.

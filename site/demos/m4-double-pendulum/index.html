Double pendulum: chaos

Two double pendulums that start a billionth of a meter apart, far below anything you can see. For about ten seconds they move as one; then they have nothing to do with each other. The difference grows exponentially, so on the log plot below it climbs as a straight line from the very start. Switch to f32 vs f64 to start them identically and watch rounding alone do the same thing. Grab a bob to throw one of them off.

Divergence

Distance between the two tips, in meters (log scale). Exponential growth is a straight line here; it flattens once the tips are as far apart as the pendulums allow (a few meters).

Energy

Energy lost since release, in joules. The impulse solver keeps it within a few hundredths of a joule, so the chaos is the pendulum's, not the solver's. Switch to XPBD: its damping drains the energy and the chaos with it.

Falling sand: a cellular automaton

No forces and no floats: every cell holds a material, and each step every cell looks at its neighbours and maybe swaps with one. Piles, flows and levels come out of those local rules. Press and drag to paint; held still, the brush keeps pouring. Sand piles and sinks through water; water levels; lava flows slowly and, where it meets water, turns to stone and the water to steam, which rises and condenses. The grid is scanned bottom-up, alternating direction each row and step, so nothing leans one way; random choices are keyed by position, so a run is the same every time (docs/11-falling-sand.md). Only awake chunks (32 × 32) are updated: a settled pile costs nothing. Turn chunks off and the result is identical, only slower.

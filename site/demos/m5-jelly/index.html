Jelly: stiffness against stability

One jelly, the same particles and the same springs, enforced three ways: as force springs stepped by symplectic Euler, and as compliant constraints in XPBD and in the impulse solver. Drag the stiffness slider up. The force springs hold until their fastest vibration outruns the time step, then explode in a few frames. The constraints stay stable at any stiffness, all the way to rigid, which a force spring can't even express. Their price is damping: an implicit step bleeds energy, so stiff constraint jellies settle where the force jelly would ring. Grab any panel's jelly and throw it.

Ant foraging: trails in a shared field

The ants don't watch each other: they read and write two fields on the ground. Searching ants lay a home trail and follow the food trail; ants carrying food (orange) lay food and follow home. Each senses three points ahead and turns toward the strongest. Deposits weaken with distance from where an ant left the nest or the food, so a trail is strongest at its source: following it leads there. The fields spread and evaporate every step. Paint food, walls, or erase; move the nest. docs/15-swarm.md, S3.

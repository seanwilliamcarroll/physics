Plucked string: Karplus–Strong

A delay line full of noise, fed back through a two-point average: each trip round the loop repeats the noise (a pitch) and smooths it a little (the decay, faster for the high partials, like a real string). That's the whole instrument, computed in Rust, compiled to WebAssembly, and run on the browser's audio thread. The picture below is the loop itself: watch the noise of each pluck smooth into a tone.

Play with your computer keyboard: the home row (A S D … ;) is the white keys, the row above (W E T …) the black ones, Z / X shift an octave; or click the keys. Then turn off the fractional delay: the loop can only be a whole number of samples long, and from about G3 up most notes go audibly out of tune (the readout says by how much).

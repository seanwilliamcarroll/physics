Physarum: networks from one shared field

Jones's (2010) model of a slime mould: 100,000 agents and one field. Each agent senses the field at three points ahead, turns toward the strongest, steps forward and deposits; the field spreads (a 3 × 3 mean) and decays. What you see is the field. From a random start a web of strands forms within seconds, then contracts, loops shortening and closing (on a small wrapped-around world, measured at 128², it ends as a single straight band; try "a small world"). Click to add a nutrient node, which deposits every step; strong nodes can pull the strands between them. Turn sensing off and the field stays a fog. docs/15-swarm.md, S4.

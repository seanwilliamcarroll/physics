Collisions: a box of balls and a break

Balls as circles, contacts as one-sided constraints, the same scene stepped by XPBD and by the impulse solver side by side. In the box, gravity and four walls: at restitution 1 the total energy stays flat (within 0.1% over 20 s), below 1 it runs down until the balls lie still. In the break, a cue ball hits a rack in free space: nothing outside pushes, so the momentum holds to rounding through every collision (the rack leaves the view in a few seconds; reset racks it again). Bounces are resolved by Generalized Reflections (Smith et al. 2012): contacts hit at the same instant share one bounce, so a ball hitting two at once neither gains nor loses energy. Turn on the broadphase overlay to see the grid and the pairs it hands the narrowphase. Grab a ball and throw it.

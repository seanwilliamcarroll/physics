Pendulum: the solver duel

One pendulum (1 m rod, 1 kg bob, released level with the pin), solved two ways. The impulse solver fixes the rod's velocity, so the bob drifts off the circle unless the Baumgarte factor β feeds the error back. XPBD projects the bob's position back onto the circle, so the rod never stretches, but the projection quietly drains energy. More substeps fix most of both. The faint circle is the rod's true length.

Chains: solid vs rubbery

A heavy bob on a chain of light links, 100 times lighter, solved two ways. Grab any link or the bob and fling it. XPBD keeps every link at its length; the impulse solver lets the chain stretch and lag behind your hand. Same chain, same settings, same cost. Lower the mass ratio and the difference shrinks: it's the heavy bob the light links can't hold. More iterations help both.

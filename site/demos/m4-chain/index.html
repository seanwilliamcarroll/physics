Chains: heavy bob, light links

A heavy bob on a chain of light links, 100 times lighter, solved two ways. Grab any link or the bob and fling it. Both hold: XPBD corrects positions directly, and the impulse solver does too, through split impulses. Now switch the impulse panel to classic Baumgarte, the textbook method from the pendulum page: it feeds position error back as real velocity, and on a heavy chain that velocity piles up until the chain explodes. More substeps make it worse, the opposite of the pendulum page's lesson. Lower the mass ratio and it calms down.

Link error

The worst link's stretch, in meters (log scale): how far the chain is from its true length. The drag's own pull isn't counted.

Energy

Energy gained since the start, in joules (the swing holds about 10 J). A frictionless chain can only lose energy to a solver; a rising line is the solver pumping it in.

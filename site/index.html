Physics demos

A 2D physics engine in Rust, compiled to WebAssembly.

  • M0 · Hello particles — the pipeline: fixed-timestep simulation in wasm, Canvas 2D rendering
  • M1 · Orbits and integrators — explicit Euler, symplectic Euler, and velocity Verlet on one orbit, with their energy errors
  • M2 · N-body gravity, f32 vs f64 — the same chaotic scene in two float types, their divergence, softening, and cost growing as N²
  • M3 · Pendulum: the solver duel — impulses vs XPBD on one pendulum: rod stretch, Baumgarte, energy loss, and substeps
  • M4 · Chains: solid vs rubbery — a heavy bob on a light chain under both solvers; grab and fling it
  • M4 · Double pendulum: chaos — two pendulums a billionth of a meter apart, and f32 vs f64, diverging

Physics demos

A 2D physics engine in Rust, compiled to WebAssembly.

  • M0 · Hello particles — the pipeline: fixed-timestep simulation in wasm, Canvas 2D rendering
  • M1 · Orbits and integrators — explicit Euler, symplectic Euler, and velocity Verlet on one orbit, with their energy errors
  • M2 · N-body gravity, f32 vs f64 — the same chaotic scene in two float types, their divergence, softening, and cost growing as N²
  • M3 · Pendulum: the solver duel — impulses vs XPBD on one pendulum: rod stretch, Baumgarte, energy loss, and substeps
  • M4 · Chains: heavy bob, light links — grab and fling a heavy chain; watch classic Baumgarte blow it up
  • M4 · Double pendulum: chaos — two pendulums a billionth of a meter apart, and f32 vs f64, diverging
  • M5 · Jelly: stiffness against stability — force springs explode past a critical stiffness; constraints hold, up to rigid
  • M6 · Collisions: a box of balls and a break — energy flat at e = 1, momentum to rounding, and the broadphase's grid
  • M6 · Newton's cradle: XPBD against impulses — simultaneous impacts passed down a row; substeps, not iterations, close the gap
  • M7 · Billiards: slide, roll, and why speculative contacts — a WPA table; stun, follow and draw from the cloth; balls that tunnel without speculation
  • M9 · Falling sand: a cellular automaton — sand, water, stone, lava and steam from local rules; paint, and watch settled chunks go to sleep

Sound

Physical-modeling synthesis, in Rust and WebAssembly on the browser's audio thread.

  • A1 · Plucked string: Karplus–Strong — play a delay-line string from your keyboard; turn off its fractional delay and hear it go out of tune
  • A2 · Waveguide string: where you pluck, where you listen — two traveling waves; pluck at ½ and the even harmonics vanish, as the spectrum shows
  • A3 · Piano: stiff strings, a felt hammer — steel strings' overtones run sharp, and a harder strike is brighter: press low on a key to hit it harder
